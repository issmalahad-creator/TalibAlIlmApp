## Round 2 — qibla and adhan (same day, Ismail: «fix the qibla direction … athan timing to follow the new location»)

| Area | Found | Fixed |
|---|---|---|
| **Qibla: magnetic vs true north** | Both sensors (`flutter_compass` on Android, `flutter_rotation_sensor` everywhere) measure from **magnetic** north. The qibla bearing (`adhan_dart`) is measured from **true** north. The compass and AR were therefore off by the local declination: ~2° in Addis Ababa, 3.5° in Makkah, 4.8° in Cairo, −12° in New York, 10° in Helsinki. | `lib/services/magnetic_declination.dart`: WMM2025 (NOAA, public domain), pure Dart, 90 coefficients. It matches the independent `pygeomag` within 0.05° at 12 cities (`test/magnetic_declination_test.dart`). The correction is applied to the compass and AR headings, and the compass shows it. |
| Qibla: no feedback | The student had to stare at the dial. | One haptic tap on entering the 8° window, re-armed beyond 14°. |
| **Adhan: only today, only at startup** | Only today's remaining prayers were scheduled, and only when the app started. So on any day the app wasn't opened there was no adhan, and after travelling the times stayed those of the old city and timezone. | Prayers are now scheduled for 3 days (ids 80d1–80d5). On every return to the foreground, `refreshPrayerScheduleIfStale()` re-reads the timezone and location and reschedules when the day changed, the timezone changed, or the person moved ≥ 5 km. Location also falls back to the phone's last-known fix. |

## Rules that came out of this

1. After any `await` in a `State`, check `mounted` before calling `setState` or using `context`.
2. Never decode an asset or response larger than about 1 MB on the UI isolate; use `compute`.
3. Every network call gets a `.timeout(...)`.
4. Run `find_arabic_literals.py` on a new screen before committing it.
5. Every compass heading is magnetic until corrected with `MagneticDeclination`. Never compare it with a true bearing directly.
6. Anything tied to the person's location or day (prayer times, adhkar hours) must refresh when they come back to the app, not only at startup.
